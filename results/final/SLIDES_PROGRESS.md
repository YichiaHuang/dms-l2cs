## Slide 3 — Training Setting: Paper vs Ours

## Slide 5 — Ablation: Which Change Helped?
Chart: `figs/p2_ablation.png` (5 subjects, epoch 50)

- Augmentation only: **3.99°** (best) · Cosine only: 4.55° (worse than paper, 4.33°)
- Why cosine alone hurts: LR falls to 1e-7 and locks in overfitting; constant-LR noise acts as a regularizer
- **The gain comes from augmentation** (5 subjects only: not significant, p > 0.17)

講稿：分開測 cosine 和資料增強：只用增強最好，只用 cosine 反而變差。改善來自資料增強。

## Slide 8 — Will It Run on a Car Chip?
Chart: `figs/p4_cost.png`

- MobileNetV3-L fits every chip: 0.85 GMACs per face, ≥ 3× headroom on 1.1–8 TOPS chips, exports to ONNX
- ResNet-50 needs ≥ 8 TOPS: 16.35 GMACs; CPU int8 1 core 32 ms vs 20 ms
- Next bottleneck: face detection (≈ 76% of a frame). NPU numbers are upper bounds, not measured on a board.

講稿：虛線是各晶片在 25 fps 下每幀可用的運算量。MobileNetV3 在所有晶片都有 3 倍以上餘裕。NPU 是理論值，沒有上板實測。

## Slide 11 — A Few Target Images Go a Long Way
Charts: `figs/p6_fewshot_amount.png` (left), `figs/p7_fewshot_tradeoff.png` (right)

- **10 images per person (110 total): RT-GENE 28.6° → 12.6°** (3 subject folds) — beats the car model's 14.8°
- Most of the gain comes from the first images: fold 0 — 10/person 13.8° · 100/person 13.7° · all 80k images 13.4°
- **But it forgets:** Gaze360 11.6° → 22.5° (blue dot, top-left)
- Error = mean of 3 subject folds at the last iteration; test subjects are never seen in fine-tuning

講稿：左圖是收多少張的效果：每人 10 張就拿到大部分的進步，收全部 8 萬張只再好 0.4 度。右圖橫軸是 RT-GENE 誤差，縱軸是 Gaze360 誤差，越左下越好。只用新資料微調（藍點）會跑到左上：RT-GENE 變好，但大角度忘光了。

## Slide 12 — Replay: Keep What the Model Knew
Chart: `figs/p9_replay.png`

- **Replay** = each batch mixes r% of the original training data (Gaze360 + MPII)
- 10/person + 50% replay: no forgetting, but RT-GENE stays at 14.6° — the old data dilutes 110 new images
- **100/person + 25% replay: RT-GENE 12.5° · Gaze360 12.0° · MPII 5.5°** — passes all 3 pre-set criteria (≤ 13°, each within +0.5°)
- Honest caveat: Gaze360 margin is only 0.03°, one seed; fold 0 alone is 14.5° (still beats the car model on every fold)

講稿：replay 就是微調時每個 batch 混一部分舊資料。只有 10 張時混一半會把新資料稀釋掉。每人 100 張、混 25%，RT-GENE 12.5 度，跟不混一樣好，而且大角度和 MPII 都沒變差，三個事先定好的標準都過了。不過 Gaze360 只比上限低 0.03 度，而且只跑一個 seed，要保守看待。
